Observations & data caveats

Concentration risk on both sides. QualityKiosk Technologies alone provides 27 of 115 testers (23%) spread over 5 segments, while 3 vendors supply a single tester each — those are effectively single points of failure for the platforms they cover.

Coverage gaps. 52 of the 98 listed applications have no tester mapped to them by name in the Q3 sheet. Some are covered indirectly through grouped platform entries, but the mapping should be confirmed before it is used for capacity planning.

Workbook variance. The detail sheet lists 115 testers; the pivot summaries in the same workbook (Sheet2 / Sheet3) total 102 and use a different department grouping, including a vendor (Jocata) that does not appear in the detail rows and excluding others (Moksha, 8 testers). This page is built from the detail sheet, which is the more complete record.

Data hygiene. Vendor and segment names carry inconsistent spacing and casing in the source (e.g. “Quinnox ”, “Harjai Technologies ”, “yes” vs “Yes”); these have been normalised for the counts above. Change-request volume is recorded as free text for 8 applications (“1 BRD p/m”, “25 CR P/A” and similar) and is excluded from numeric totals.
